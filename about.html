Corporate profile

Sarah George Gordon (Sarah Gordon) is an experienced full stack developer based in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania, and cofounder of TechMorah Solution LTD.

My work spans four practice areas: software development (Laravel, Flutter, Spring Boot, React/Next.js), design & graphics (logos, posters, brand identity, UI systems, social and print creatives), implementation & support (banking channels, SQL Server, Windows Server/IIS, Linux VPS, go-live training), and documents & reports (proposals, presentations, SSRS reports, integration specs, and handover runbooks).

Recent delivery includes Active Targets e-commerce, imartListener + iMart SMS portal, the full LipaPay product family, Savanna Fibre, school fee systems, Victoria Lush SMS on Linux VPS, ELMS leave, and brand work for logistics, fintech, and SME clients. I operate comfortably from brief through design, build, deploy, and long-term support.
